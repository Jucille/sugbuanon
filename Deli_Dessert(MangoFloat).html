MANGO FLOAT

What is Mango Float?

Mango float or crema de mangga is a Filipino icebox cake dessert made with layers of ladyfingers. It is chilled for a few hours before serving, though it can also be frozen to give it an ice cream-like consistency.

What made Mango Float famous?

- Its famous because of its refreshing and delicous taste. The dessert is a modern variant of the traditional Filipino crema de fruta cake, and it is also known by various other names like mango royale or crema de mangga.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 5 mangoes, ripe but not soft
  • 4 cups heavy cream(960 mL), or canned all-purpose cream, chilled overnight
  • 1 can condensed milk, chilled overnight
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 21 crackers graham cracker, plus more, crushed, for topping

STEPS:

  1. Whip the cream until it forms stiff peaks. You can use an electric mixer or a whisk for this.
  2. Gently fold in the condensed milk into the whipped cream. Adjust the amount of condensed milk according to your taste.
  3. In a serving dish or container, arrange a layer of graham crackers at the bottom. You may need to break them into smaller pieces to fit the dish.
  4. Spread a layer of the whipped cream mixture over the graham crackers.
  5. Place a layer of sliced mangoes on top of the whipped cream mixture.
  6. Repeat the layers of graham crackers, whipped cream mixture, and mango slices until you fill the dish or reach your desired height.
  7. Finish with a layer of whipped cream on top and decorate with additional mango slices if desired.
  8. Cover the dish with plastic wrap or foil and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight to allow the flavors to meld and the graham crackers to soften.
  9. Serve chilled and enjoy your delicious mango float!
