PUTO BUMBONG

What is Puto Bumbong?

Puto Bumbong is a type of Filipino, it is a steamed purple rice cake that is traditionally prepared by steaming a mixture of ground purple rice inside a bamboo tube. The rice cake is typically topped with margarine or butter, freshly grated coconut, and muscovado sugar. The color of traditional puto bumbong comes from the rice itself, and food coloring is not traditionally used. The rice should also give the dish a subtle taste that is enhanced by the toppings.

What made Puto Bumbong famous?

- Its popularity of puto bumbong during the Christmas season made it famous, its unique preparation method, and its distinct taste and appearance have all contributed to its fame. Additionally, the cultural significance of puto bumbong as a traditional Filipino Christmas food has helped to keep it popular and well-known.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup glutinous rice flour
  • 6 tablespoon water
  • ¼ teaspoon violet liquid food color
  • ½ cup grated coconut
  • 1 tablespoon Butter or margarine for brushing
  • 3 tablespoon Brown sugar or muscovado
  • Grated cheese optional

    STEPS:

  1. Shape cuts of foil into thin long containers, about 4 inches long and ¾ inch thick and high. Make about 8-10.
  2. In a small bowl, combine water and food color and mix.
  3. Place glutinous rice flour on a separate bowl. Add colored water 2 tablespoon at a time and mix it well using a spoon after every addition.
  4. Once all liquid is added, mix using hand while breaking clumps with your fingers into tiny crumbs or grains.
  5. Brush the shaped foils with butter or margarine generously. Fill each container with the glutinous rice mix.
  6. Steam for 10-15 minutes. Turn off heat and let them cool down a bit.
  7. Invert each piece on a banana leaf brushed with butter or margarine upto 4-5 logs per leaf.
  8. Brush the top and sides with butter or margarine, then top with grated coconut and brown sugar or muscovado. Add grated cheese or roasted sesame seeds if desired.
