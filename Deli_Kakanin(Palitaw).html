What is Palitaw?

Palitaw is a flattened oval cake made from malagkit rice paste, also used to make bilo-bilo for ginataan. The kakanin gets heated in water and scraped out when it reaches the surface. It then gets dusted with sugar and covered with shredded coconut.

What made Palitaw famous?

- Palitaw is a famous Filipino dessert or snack that is made from glutinous rice flour. The name "palitaw" is derived from the Filipino word "litaw," which means "to float," as the rice cakes rise to the surface when they are cooked in boiling water.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups glutinous rice flour
    • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup water
  • ½ cup sesame seeds roasted
  • 1 cup grated coconut

STEPS:

  1. Combine glutinous rice flour with water and mix until a dough is formed.
  2. Scoop about 2 tablespoons of dough then mold it into a ball-shaped figure.
  3. Flatten the ball-shaped dough using the palm of your hands.
  4. Boil water in a cooking pot then put-in the flattened dough.
  5. When the flattened dough starts to float, remove them from the pot and set it aside allowing water to drip.
  6. Combine sugar and roasted sesame seeds then mix well.
  7. Roll the rice cake in grated coconut then in the sugar-sesame seed mixture.
  8. Arrange in a serving plate then serve.
