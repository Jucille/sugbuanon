DISH

LECHON

What is Lechon?

Lechon, a roasted pig dish, is particularly famous in Cebu, Philippines. The unique way of preparing and cooking lechon in Cebu sets it apart from other regions in the country. The pig is stuffed with a mix of herbs and spices, such as lemongrass, bay leaves, and peppercorns, and then roasted slowly over charcoal.

What made Lechon famous?

- Its method of cooking results in a crispy skin and flavorful meat that is aromatic and delicious. Lechon is a traditional meal for holidays, get-togethers, and fiesta celebrations in Cebu. Its popularity has also spread beyond the region, with visitors from around the world coming to Cebu to try the famous dish. In fact, renowned chef Anthony Bourdain even declared Cebu lechon as the "best pig ever."

    INGREDIENTS

  • Pork
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Lemongrass
  • Bay Leaves
  • Salt
  • Soy Sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Calamansi

    STEPS:

  1. Choose a fresh and high-quality pig, preferably around 10-15 kg for a manageable size.
  2. Clean the pig thoroughly, removing any hair follicles and innards. Rinse well and pat dry.
  3. Season the pig inside and out with salt, pepper, and soy sauce. Stuff the cavity with lemongrass, onions, and garlic
  4. Truss the pig securely with kitchen twine, ensuring that the legs and arms are tied close to the body.
  5. Prepare a roasting pit with hot charcoal, arranging it for indirect heat cooking.
  6. Place the pig on a spit and position it over the pit.
  7. Roast the pig for 3-4 hours, turning it regularly to ensure even cooking.
  8. Baste the pig with a mixture of vinegar, soy sauce, and calamansi to keep it moist and flavorful.
