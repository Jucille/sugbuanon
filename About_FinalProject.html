Why did we make this website?

  • We made this website to promote local cuisine. A website about food in Cebu can help promote the unique and delicious local cuisine to a wider audience. This can attract more tourists to the region and support local businesses.

  • To Share Information. This website can serve as a platform to share information about the best places to eat in Cebu, including restaurants, street food stalls, and markets. This can help visitors and locals alike discover new places to eat and enjoy the local cuisine.

  • Build a Community. This food website can also help build a community of food lovers in Cebu. This community can share recipes, restaurant recommendations, and food experiences, fostering a sense of connection and camaraderie among its members.
