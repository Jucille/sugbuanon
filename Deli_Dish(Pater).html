DISH

PATER

What is Pater?

Pater is a dish that originated in Mindanao, specifically in Maguindanao Province. This dish is also commonly known as pastil, patir, or patel. It is rice with shredded meat toppings like beef, chicken, or tuna, and it is wrapped in banana leaves.

What made Pater famous?

- Pater food became famous in Cebu due to its convenience, affordability, and delicious taste.The dish is often wrapped in banana leaves and can be made with chicken, beef, or tuna. It is commonly served with palapa, a spicy condiment made from shallots, ginger, and other spices. Several restaurants in Cebu, such as Doi Cravin’s, Pastil Baii, Twin Pastel, Pater sa Tisa, and Paterholic, have contributed to its fame by offering authentic and delicious pater dishes. These establishments have helped bring the taste of Mindanao closer to Cebuanos, making pater a well-loved dish in the region.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  • 1/2 cup glutinous rice
  • lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Banana leaves (optional)

    STEPS:

  1. Rinse the jasmine and glutinous rice together, then cook according to the package instructions.
  2. While the rice is cooking, boil the chicken breast until it's cooked through. Once cooked, shred the chicken using two forks.
  3. In a pan, sauté the onion and garlic until they're soft and fragrant. Add the shredded chicken, soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Cook for a few more minutes until the chicken is well-coated with the sauce.
  4. Once the rice is done, mix it with the chicken mixture.
  5. If you're using banana leaves, wrap the rice and chicken mixture in a leaf and serve. If not, you can just serve it on a plate.
