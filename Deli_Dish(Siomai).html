SIOMAI

What is siomai?

Pork Siomai is a traditional Chinese dumpling. This popular dumpling has made its way to the heart of the Filipino's as evidenced by the hundreds of stalls.

What made Siomai famous?

- Siomai is among the Chinese dishes that Filipinos embraced. It comes in different flavors and types of meat. Among the top reasons many love this dish is that you can serve it ay any occasion.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups ground pork
  • 1/2 pc onion, minced
  • 4 tbsp spring onions, chopped
  • 1/4 cup carrots, minced
  • 1/4 cup singkamas, finely minced
  • 1 pc Knorr Pork or Beef Cube, mashed
  • 3 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 pc egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • wonton wrappers, as needed
  • chopped spring onions and carrots for topping

STEPS:

  1. Prepare the filling: In a large bowl, combine ground pork, shrimp, onion, garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, sugar, cornstarch, and water. Mix until all ingredients are well blended.
  2. Wrap the siomai: Place a wonton wrapper on a flat surface. Put a tablespoon or more of the filling in the center of the wrapper. Gather the edges of the wrapper and press it around the filling, leaving the top part exposed. Repeat until all the filling is used up.
  3. Steam the siomai: Arrange the wrapped siomai in a steamer, making sure they don't touch each other. Steam for 15-20 minutes or until cooked through.
  4. Serve: Serve the siomai with soy sauce, calamansi, and chili garlic paste on the side. Enjoy!
